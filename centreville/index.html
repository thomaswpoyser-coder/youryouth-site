A church family in the heart of the Annapolis Valley. Whoever you are, and wherever you are starting from, there is a place for you here.

Our week

Sundays

Worship Service

10:30 AM

We gather every Sunday morning to sing, pray, and open the Bible together. Come as you are — you will be made welcome.

Tuesdays

Community Coffee & Conversation

9:30 – 11:00 AM

An open door and a hot cup of coffee. Neighbours, friends, and the simply curious — everyone is welcome at the table.

Fridays

SoulFire Youth

6:30 – 8:30 PM

Our youth group meets Friday nights for games, friendship, and honest talk about faith and life. New faces are always welcome.

New here?

You do not need to have it all together, and you do not need to know anyone. Come to a Sunday service, or start smaller — coffee on a Tuesday morning is a gentle first step.

To hear what is happening week to week, ask us about Sharing The Good Word, our church newsletter.

Our Pastor

Pastor Emmanuel — most of us call him Pastor Emma — would be glad to meet you, answer questions, or simply pray with you.
