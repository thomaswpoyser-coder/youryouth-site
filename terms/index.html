Acceptable use

Members may not bully, threaten, or harass anyone; pressure anyone to keep secrets, meet alone, or share photos; post sexual content; or use the app for anything unlawful. Adults may not use Your Youth to move a conversation with a minor off the platform. Leaders may remove members, and we may suspend or close accounts or groups that break these terms.

Screening and moderation

Messages, questions, and prayer requests are screened automatically for safety patterns, and flagged content is held for the group's leaders, who decide what happens next. Screening assists leaders; it cannot catch everything, and a flag is never proof of wrongdoing. Leaders can also remove content and members at any time.
