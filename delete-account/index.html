Delete your account

You can delete your Your Youth account, and the personal data attached to it, at any time. There are two ways:

In the app (immediate)

  1. Open Your Youth and tap the small gear at the top right to open Settings
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account
  3. Type DELETE to confirm

Your account, profile and sign-in are removed right away, and so is what you posted — your chat messages, questions and prayer requests are deleted with the account.

By email

If you can't sign in, email youth@cbckc.ca from the email address on the account and ask us to delete it. We will confirm once it is done, within 30 days at the latest and usually much sooner.

If you created a group, deleting your account does not delete the group — but it leaves it without an owner, so transfer ownership to another leader inside the app first if you want it to keep one. Questions: youth@cbckc.ca. See also our privacy policy.
