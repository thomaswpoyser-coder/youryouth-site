Everything a youth group needs, nothing it doesn't

A leader creates the group, uploads its logo, and hands out a join code — then youth, leaders and parents each get the view that's right for them.

  • Looks like your group — your logo sets the colours and cover.
  • One group chat — everyone in a single conversation, leaders in it too.
  • Anonymous questions — no name shown; leaders see who asked.
  • Prayer — requests go to leaders, never the whole group.
  • Events & RSVPs — see what's on; one tap says you're coming.
  • Lessons — the week's teaching and passage, in the app.
  • Bible built in — reading plans and passages, WEB and KJV.
  • Announcements — what's new reaches the whole group at once.
  • Approvals — a leader approves every student and parent who joins.
  • Safety queue — flagged messages are held for a leader's review.
  • Recently deleted — deleted chat stays visible to leaders for 24 hours.
  • Sermon help — extra help with sermons and lessons, when you want it.
  • The desk — a web workbench for lessons, your public page, and queues.
  • Your public page — your group's own page, built from your group.

Safeguarding isn't a feature. It's the foundation.

Designed by people who run a real youth group, for the thing that matters most: keeping young people safe while they connect. These rules live in how the app stores its data, not just how the screens look — they can't be switched off or wandered around.

No private messaging

Two students cannot message each other one-to-one. Every conversation happens in the open, in front of the whole group and its leaders.

Screened before it's seen

Chat messages, questions, and prayer requests are screened as they're sent — for secrecy, meeting alone, pressure for photos, bullying, and crisis signals. Anything flagged is held back from students and goes to the leaders: a leader decides every time.

Parent approval for under-18s

A student under 18 can't join a group or post anything until a parent — or a leader of the group — has approved their account.

Anonymous to the group, never to leaders

Questions appear with no name attached, so youth can ask honestly. Leaders can always see who asked — anonymous never means unsupervised.

Prayer stays private

Prayer requests go to leaders only — never to the group feed, never anywhere another student can see them.

Money is grown-ups only

Giving and tips are for parents and adults. Students never see a pay button, never see who gave, and tip totals are never shown anywhere in the app.

Getting a group in is simple

1

A leader sets up the group

Create the group, upload its logo, and the app takes on the group's own look — colours, cover art, and all.

2

Everyone joins with a code

The leader shares one join code. Youth sign up, parents approve under-18 accounts, and the group is together.

3

Each person gets their view

Youth get Home, Bible, Community, and My Group. Leaders get Leader Space — safety queue, questions, prayer, events, and the group's look. Parents get approvals, notices, and giving.

Tips keep Your Youth going

Your Youth is built and run by a small team in Nova Scotia, Canada. Parents and other adults can leave a voluntary tip in the app — $2, $5, $10, or their own amount — to help cover the cost of building and running it.

How tips work+

Tips are a thank-you, not a purchase: nothing is sold in exchange for them, and giving to a group's own funds is kept separate from the tip jar. Payments aren't live yet — they're being activated now; once they are, cards are processed securely by Stripe and Your Youth never sees or stores your card number. Tip totals are never displayed in the app.

Common questions

Is Your Youth available yet?+

Built and in private testing, readying for a beta with its first group, SoulFire Youth. Store releases follow once the beta is complete and developer accounts are in place.

Who is Your Youth for?+

Christian youth groups first — the youth in them, their leaders, and their parents. Any youth group can create its own branded home; Bible content and reading plans come with it.

Which phones does it run on?+

Android and iPhone. One app, built for both.

Can students message each other privately?+

No — and that's deliberate. One group chat leaders can see, anonymous questions leaders can trace, and prayer requests only leaders receive. One-to-one messaging between students doesn't exist in Your Youth.

Which Bible translations are included?+

The World English Bible (WEB) and the King James Version (KJV). Both are public domain, so they're free for every group with no licensing limits.

How does a group get started?+

A leader creates the group in the app, uploads the group's logo, and shares the join code. Write to youth@cbckc.ca to talk about bringing your group on.
